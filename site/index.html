Set up Snag

Paste a link.

Snag turns an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube link into one line for Terminal. The video downloads on your own Mac.

Several links at once is fine. Separate them with a space.

          Paste it into Terminal
          Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Enter. Paste with ⌘ V and press Enter again.
        

        

          First time on this Mac?
          The command needs Snag installed first. It is a one-time setup of about 10 minutes.

It lands ready to edit.

      

        
By default Snag takes the sharpest version the site offers in H.264, the format Premiere, Resolve and QuickTime open without converting. Most download sites hand you AV1 or VP9 instead, which plays fine and scrubs badly.

        
Need 4K that still edits? 4K (slow) downloads the best stream and re-encodes it to H.264 on your Mac. It takes minutes, not seconds.
