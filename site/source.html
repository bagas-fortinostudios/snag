Set up Snag

Read it before you run it.

You should not paste an installer from a stranger's website unread. So here is all of it.

Copy the source and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or whichever assistant you use, with a question like “Is this safe to run on my Mac, and what exactly does it do?” You do not have to read shell to check something written in it.

When you are satisfied, copy the installer, paste it into Terminal, and open a new tab.

The installer (shortened here, the copy is complete)

        

      

      

        
Writes
~/bin/snag, plus one line in ~/.zshrc if your path needs it

        
Length
 lines of shell

        
Connects to
The site you give it, through yt-dlp. snag dash also fetches a short YouTube video for its self-test.

        
Needs
macOS, Homebrew, ffmpeg, yt-dlp

      

    


    

      

        
        ~/bin/snag
        
      

      

    

  






  

    

      
Snag is a wrapper around yt-dlp and ffmpeg, who do the actual work.
