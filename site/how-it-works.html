Set up Snag

Three tools.
One short command.

A free way to save videos from Instagram, TikTok and YouTube onto your own Mac. Set it up once, then it is one line every time.

yt-dlp fetches the video

The part that actually downloads from the site. Open source, and fixed within days whenever a site changes.

ffmpeg builds the file

Joins the picture and the sound into one finished file, and converts it when you ask for a different format.

snag picks the settings

On their own, those two need a long line of settings that changes from site to site, and it is easy to get wrong. Snag reads your link, picks the settings, and runs the whole job in one go.

How a download works

The first four steps happen in your browser. The last one is the only thing you do in Terminal.

  1. Find the video

    Instagram, TikTok, a YouTube Short or a full YouTube video.

  2. Copy the link

    The app's Share or Copy link button, or your browser's address bar.

  3. Paste it into Snag

    On the Overview page. Pick a format.

  4. Get the command

    One line, copied for you.

  5. Paste into Terminal

    Press Enter. The file lands in Downloads.

Four formats. One is the default for a reason.

Each one says exactly what lands in your Downloads folder.

Ready to edit

The best version the site offers in H.264. Opens in Premiere, Resolve and QuickTime with no converting. Tops out at 1080p on YouTube, because that is where H.264 stops there.

H.264 · AAC · MP4snag

Small file

The same format, capped at 720p. For sending, previewing or keeping.

720p · H.264 · MP4snag -q 720

Audio only

Drops the picture. Cover art and track details are written into the file.

MP3 · cover artsnag --mp3

4K (slow)

The sharpest stream available, usually AV1 above 1080p, re-encoded to H.264 on your Mac so it edits. Takes real minutes, not seconds.

Best stream → H.264 CRF 18snag --max --recode

Why the default matters. Sorting by codec before resolution quietly caps you at 1080p, and sorting by resolution alone hands you AV1 or VP9, which plays fine and scrubs badly in an editor. Snag filters to H.264 first, then takes the highest resolution of those.

What it will not do

Better to know now than after setup.

  • Run on Windows or Linux
  • Download from this website
  • Upload anything to the internet
  • Add ads, watermarks or a daily limit

Built on Homebrew, yt-dlp and ffmpeg.
