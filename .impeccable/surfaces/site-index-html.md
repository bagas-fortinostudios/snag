## Direction contract

THESIS: A tool entry that reviews itself honestly, then hands you the coupon to order it. The page's authority comes from stating what the tool is not (not a service, not better than yt-dlp, not able to touch your machine from a browser) before stating what it is. It refuses the category default: the centred dark terminal hero with a one-line install box and a copy button, which asks for trust it has not earned from a stranger.

OWN-WORLD: Cheap-offset mail-order tool catalog, 1968-74. Ground is pulp stock, grey-olive and deliberately not cream; ink is heavy near-black; one process cyan carries whole regions, not accents; a cheap vermilion is reserved for stamps and marks only. Dark theme is the same sheet as a press proof, ink and stock inverted, never a different world. Commands sit in dark halftone plates the way a catalog sets its photographs. Chivo for display and UI, Source Serif 4 for the honest-evaluation prose, Courier Prime for commands and marginalia. Rules and stamps do the separating; there is no card system.

STORY: A stranger arrives from a shared link believing this is another downloader website. They understand within one screen that nothing uploads, that the work happens on their own Mac, and that the site is telling them the unflattering parts first. They believe it because the whole script is printed on the page before anyone asks them to run it. They complete setup once, then tick a coupon and paste one line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A masthead rule across full width, the name set in Chivo 900 caps at display scale, and directly beneath it a Courier Prime colophon line carrying version, cost, and platform. No hero image and no install box. Immediately below the rule, the first entry begins mid-evaluation so the honest paragraph is readable without scrolling, with the left access rail already showing what you need and where it comes from. The order coupon's top edge and its first tick boxes are visible at the fold on desktop.

FORM: Access-to-Tools Catalog, candidate 4 of seven on my ordered grounded list, assigned by the roll. Seed key 55c487be. Raised by four named donations: state as a mark rather than a hue, from the cutting bench rail; colour as the sheet rather than the mark, from the copy-shop zine; every option present at once with the chosen one struck forward, from the struck cathode gauze; one imperative per sheet, from the WPA park poster. Signature interaction is the order coupon: ticking a box lands as a stamp, not a tween, and the printed command re-sets. Motion grammar is stamped and instant, nothing longer than 90ms, reduced-motion drops to no movement at all.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
