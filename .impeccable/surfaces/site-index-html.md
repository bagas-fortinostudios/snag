Scope: the public Snag site, now five static pages (index, how-it-works, setup, help, source) sharing one stylesheet. Home is Persuade with an Operate core (the paste box); the other four are Read.

Audience and job: a Mac user who has never opened Terminal arrives from a shared link, pastes a video link, copies one command. Post people care that the file cuts without transcoding.

Constraints: the site cannot download; it builds a command. No invented claims, numbers or testimonials. Light only. macOS only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Snag is a Mac utility, so the site is a Mac: one search-style field that turns a pasted link into the exact Terminal line, with every explanation moved to its own page behind a frosted nav. Brief-pinned by Bagas (Apple.com plus macOS, light only, search-box hero, SaaS nav), replacing the 1968 catalog sheet; the roll was not run because the user pinned the world and the arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Apple.com and macOS Sonoma light. Near-white #fbfbfd ground under a soft blue, lilac and peach light field; frosted white glass (blur 30px, saturate 180%, white hairline, soft offset shadow) for nav, field, popovers and windows; one accent, system blue #0071e3. SF Pro via the system stack, SF Mono for commands. Commands live in light macOS Terminal windows with traffic lights, not dark plates. Pills, segmented controls and macOS switches.

STORY: The visitor sees one field and understands the job in a second. They paste, pick a format, press Get command. A Terminal window drops in with their line, already copied, and a plain line saying what lands in Downloads and that setup comes first if this is their first time.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass nav across the top (wordmark left, five links centre, Set up pill right). Centred headline at about 72 to 80px, a sub of at most two balanced lines, then the paste field at 680px wide as the largest object on the page, format segmented control beneath it, supported-site row under that. The result window appears below the field.

FORM: canon, played straight at Apple.com fidelity, user-pinned; first on my list. Seed key: none. Seed authority is Bagas's pinned answers of 2026-10-13: reference "Apple.com + macOS", paste box "Build command + copy", theme "Light only", plus his request for a search-engine box hero and a SaaS top nav. Signature interaction: paste, the field reads the site and swaps its icon, Get command drops a Terminal window in with a spring and copies the line. Motion: one spring reveal, 420ms ease-out, reduced motion drops to an instant swap.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
